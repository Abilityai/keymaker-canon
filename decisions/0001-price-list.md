# 0001 - The price list and the discount rule

- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Decided by:** the principal
- **What:** four offers (audit $2,500; pilot $12,000; build from $40,000; operate $2,000/month) are
  the list. A rep may give up to 10% on a pilot or a build, as an invoice credit; above that, the
  principal decides. No discounts on the audit.
- **Why:** a discount given as a rate cut becomes the client's reference price for everything
  after it; an invoice credit does not. The audit is priced to be bought without a negotiation.
- **Touches:** `pricing.md`, `operators.yaml` (approvals), the sales desk's proposals.
