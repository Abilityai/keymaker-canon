# 0005 - We serve specialist recruiting firms

**Decision.** Keymaker sells to one kind of client: **specialist recruiting firms of 20-100
people** - agency recruiters placing permanent and contract hires in one field (finance, tech,
healthcare, engineering), paid a placement fee. Who they are, what they buy and how we talk to
them lives in `domains/market/who-we-serve.md`. Every offer on the price list is described for
this client; prices do not change.

**Why.** A recruiting firm's economics fit in one line: a placement fee of roughly 20-25% of
first-year salary, earned by recruiters who spend most of their week on work agents do well -
sourcing lists, candidate summaries, shortlists to the client, weekly updates, keeping candidates
warm. Hours given back become placements, so the value is countable. And everyone has hired or
been placed by a recruiter, so nobody needs the business explained.

**What it touches.** The website (hero, offer copy, the form), the sales desk (who it qualifies
and how it opens a conversation), marketing (who content is for), and the workshop pages.

**What does not change.** Prices (`domains/pricing/pricing.md`), approvals, and routing (decision
0003). Clients signed before this decision - in any field - are served exactly as before; this
decides who we look for, not who we keep. A request from outside the field is still answered:
Sales qualifies it and may decline politely.

**Kill criteria.** Revisit at review if, by 2026-12-03, no recruiting firm has reached stage 2
(Qualified), or if two requests from one other field outnumber recruiting requests.
